Our Campus & Tradition

Rooted in Darjeeling's Renowned Tradition of Education.

Located at 6th Mile, Naya Busty in Dist. Darjeeling, West Bengal, Takdah Grace Academy stands as a beacon of quality schooling for families across Takdah Cantonment, Rangliot, Tinchuley, and adjoining tea estates.

The hill district of Darjeeling has long been globally celebrated for its prestigious educational institutions. Takdah Grace Academy upholds this fine legacy by combining the rigorous standards of the ICSE curriculum with affordable fees, attentive mentoring, modern digital teaching tools, and traditional values of courtesy and respect.
